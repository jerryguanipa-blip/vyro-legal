03 · How it works

From your gallery to your Status, in five steps.

No account needed. The code step is the one unusual part, and it has a reason.

  1. 01

    Pick the video

    Choose it from your gallery or record it in the app.

  2. 02

    It is optimized on your phone

    VYRO turns it into vertical Full HD and, if it is long, splits it into parts. Your original never leaves the device.

  3. 03

    WhatsApp opens with a code

    The app opens the VYRO chat with a single-use code already typed. You only have to send it.

  4. 04

    The video comes back to that chat

    The server returns the optimized video in the same conversation.

  5. 05

    Post it to your Status

    Forward the video to “My status”.

Why the code exists

It tells the server which video to deliver and to whom; it works once and expires after 30 minutes. The video comes back by chat so WhatsApp treats it as a received file and does not recompress it twice. When you post the Status, WhatsApp applies its final compression and VYRO cannot remove it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the code sent through WhatsApp?

It is a random single-use code that expires after 30 minutes. It tells VYRO which video to deliver and to whom. If it expires, tap the button in the app again to generate a new one.

Why does my Status look different from the optimized video?

Because WhatsApp compresses videos again when they are posted to Status and no external app can prevent it. VYRO gives it the best possible starting file; the final result depends on the video, the phone and what WhatsApp does.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. If you have both apps installed, VYRO asks which one you want to receive the video in.

How do I cancel a paid plan?

From Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions. Cancellations and refunds are handled by Google Play; you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

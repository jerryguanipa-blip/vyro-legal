The problem

WhatsApp processes the videos you post to your Status.

Preparing the source file well can help you get better results. VYRO takes care of that preparation.

  1. 1

    Your recording

    4K, 60 fps or very large videos that WhatsApp does not need as they are.

  2. 2

    VYRO

    Turns it into vertical Full HD with a suitable size and structure, and splits it into parts.

  3. 3

    Your Status

    WhatsApp applies its own final compression when you post. VYRO does not remove it.

VYRO cannot avoid the compression WhatsApp applies when you post a Status. What it does is give WhatsApp the best possible starting file.

How it works

Four steps, from your gallery to your Status.

Everything happens between the app and your WhatsApp. No account required.

  1. 01

    Select

    Pick a video from your gallery or record one in the app.

  2. 02

    Optimize

    VYRO prepares the video right on your device.

  3. 03

    Receive

    The VYRO chat opens with a code ready to send. When you send it, you receive the optimized file.

  4. 04

    Post

    Forward the video from that chat to “My status”.

Privacy

Your originals stay on your phone.

Optimization is local. Only if you choose to receive the video on WhatsApp is the already-optimized file temporarily transmitted to our server so it can be delivered to you.

  • On your phone

    Your original videos and photos are read and processed on the device. They are not uploaded.

  • In transit, temporarily

    The optimized video travels encrypted (HTTPS) to our server in the United States and is delivered on WhatsApp through Twilio and Meta’s platform.

  • Automatic deletion

    It is deleted once delivery is confirmed and, at most, in about an hour. The single-use code expires after 30 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

Your original never leaves your phone: optimization happens on the device. Only if you choose delivery on WhatsApp is the already-optimized video uploaded, temporarily and encrypted, and it is deleted automatically once delivery is confirmed and, at most, in about an hour.

What is the code sent through WhatsApp?

It is a random single-use code that expires after 30 minutes. It tells VYRO which video to deliver and to whom. If it expires, tap the button in the app again to generate a new one.

Why does my Status look different from the optimized video?

WhatsApp compresses videos again when they are posted to Status. VYRO gives you the best possible starting file, but that final compression is applied by WhatsApp and no external app can avoid it.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. If you have both apps installed, VYRO asks which one you want to receive the video in.

How long does it take to optimize a video?

It depends on the video and your phone. In our test with a 57-second 4K video on a Galaxy S25 Ultra it took about 42 seconds. You can minimize the app: processing continues in the background.

Is there an iPhone version?

Today VYRO is an Android app. There is no iPhone version and no App Store listing.

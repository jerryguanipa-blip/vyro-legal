Getting started

What is VYRO and what does it do?

VYRO is an Android app that optimizes your videos so they reach your WhatsApp Status in the best possible shape: it turns them into vertical Full HD with a suitable size and, if they are long, splits them into parts.

Optimization happens on your phone. Afterwards you can receive the result in a WhatsApp chat and forward it to your Status.

How do I use VYRO step by step?
  1. Open VYRO and pick a video from your gallery or record one in the app.
  2. Optionally adjust trim, text, music or audio.
  3. Tap to optimize and wait for it to finish; you can minimize the app.
  4. Tap the button to send to WhatsApp. The VYRO chat opens with a code already typed in.
  5. Send that message. A moment later you will receive the optimized video in the chat.
  6. Forward the video from that chat to “My status”.
Is there an iPhone version?

No. Today VYRO is an Android-only app and there is no App Store listing.

Where do I download VYRO?

VYRO is not yet published on Google Play. When it is, the official button will appear on the home page. Be wary of files or links presented as VYRO on other sites.

WhatsApp delivery

What is the code that appears in the WhatsApp chat?

It is a random single-use code valid for 30 minutes. It tells VYRO which video to deliver and to whom. Do not share or edit it: just send the message as it appears.

The code expired or I don’t receive the video

Codes expire after 30 minutes and work only once. If that time has passed, or you already used it, tap the send-to-WhatsApp button in the app again to generate a new one.

Also check that you sent the message from the WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) you chose in the app and that your phone has an internet connection.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. If you have both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business installed, VYRO asks which one you want to receive the video in.

How do I post the video to my Status?

When the video arrives in the VYRO chat, long-press the message, choose forward and select “My status”. If the video was split into parts, post each part as consecutive Statuses.

Can I share without using the server?

Yes. The app includes a share option through Android’s share menu that hands the file directly to WhatsApp without passing through any VYRO server. In that case WhatsApp will compress the file again when sending it.

Quality and parts

Why does my Status look different from the optimized video?

WhatsApp applies its own compression when you post a video to Status. VYRO cannot remove or avoid it; what it does is give WhatsApp a well-prepared starting file: vertical Full HD, 30 fps and a suitable structure.

The final result depends on the video, your phone and what WhatsApp does at that moment.

Why is my video split into parts?

WhatsApp Status has a maximum duration per post, so VYRO splits long videos into equal parts. On FREE the parts are 30 seconds; on PRO you can choose 45- or 60-second parts.

60-second parts are exported at 720p to keep the file within WhatsApp’s size limit; 45-second parts keep 1080p.

What resolution do I get?

Up to 1080 × 1920 (vertical Full HD) when your source video has that resolution or higher. VYRO does not invent detail: if the original has a lower resolution it is not artificially enlarged.

Processing is slow or looks stuck

Time depends on the video’s size and length and on your phone. In a test with a 57-second 4K video on a Galaxy S25 Ultra it took about 42 seconds.

If you minimize the app, processing continues in the background and you will get a notification when it finishes. Avoid force-closing the app while it processes.

Plans and payments

How does the “VYRO FREE” watermark work?

Videos on the FREE plan include a discreet “VYRO FREE” watermark. With an active PRO subscription, videos are processed without the watermark.

How do I subscribe to VYRO PRO and what does it include?

From the PRO screen in the app you will see the available plans with the price, currency and conditions Google Play shows for your account, including any free trial if you are eligible.

PRO removes the watermark and enables 45- and 60-second parts while the subscription is active.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the PRO screen tap “Restore purchase”. VYRO asks Google Play with your Google account and reactivates your subscription if it is still valid. You must use the same Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are cancelled from Google Play, not from the app: open Play Store, tap your profile picture, go to Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose VYRO PRO and tap Cancel subscription. You keep PRO access until the end of the already-paid period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies. You can request one from your Google Play order history. VYRO has no access to your payment data.

Privacy and permissions

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

Your originals are never uploaded: they are processed on your phone. Only if you choose to receive the result on WhatsApp is the already-optimized video uploaded, temporarily and encrypted, and it is deleted once delivery is confirmed and, at most, in about an hour.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why does it ask for gallery, camera or notification permissions?

Gallery: to pick the video or photo to optimize. Camera and microphone: only if you record a new video in the app. Notifications: to tell you when processing finishes. You can review or revoke them in Android settings.

How do I delete my data?

VYRO does not keep your number or your videos beyond delivery. If you want to request deletion of anything tied to a code, write to contacto@vyrohd.com. Your optimized videos stay in your gallery until you delete them.

Common issues

I don’t hear the completion notification sound

This is a known case on some devices: processing finishes correctly and the notification appears, but the sound may not play depending on your phone’s sound settings. Check that VYRO’s notifications have sound enabled under Settings, Apps, VYRO, Notifications.

The app says there is no connection to the server

Check that your phone has internet and try again: the first connection after a period of inactivity can take a while. If it continues, use the share option through Android’s share menu or write to us.

3FREE and PRO plans

  • FREE: lets you optimize videos at no cost. Videos include the “VYRO FREE” watermark and are split into 30-second parts.
  • PRO: a subscription that removes the watermark and enables 45- and 60-second parts for as long as the subscription is active according to the status reported by Google Play. 60-second parts are exported at 720p to keep the file within WhatsApp’s limit.
  • Usage limits: to protect the service, each plan may have a reasonable limit on deliveries per day, week or month. The current limits are shown in the app and may be adjusted with notice. When a limit is reached the code stays valid and you can try again when the period renews.
